Scan Mode
Pick the mode first. Then hit Scan Stocks.
DAY (Full Power)
Best accuracy for true squeezes.
Costs ~$1.87/scan
Uses Polygon + ORTEX (during ORTEX hours) to confirm real short pressure.
  • Pros: Higher confidence, confirmed squeeze metrics (SI/CTB/Avail).
  • Cons: Costs ORTEX credits each scan.
  • Best for: Morning + regular session trading.
NIGHT (Afterhours)
Cheaper scouting + watchlists.
Costs $0 ORTEX
Uses Polygon only. No ORTEX calls (so it’s cheaper).
  • Pros: Free ORTEX, fast scouting.
  • Cons: No confirmed short data → confidence is lower.
  • Best for: Building watchlists after hours.

Portfolio construction

Personalized allocation planner.

Describe the objective and constraints. The planner turns capital, risk, horizon, sectors, cash reserve, and current holdings into an allocation framework.

What these portfolio inputs mean

Total capital is the amount available to allocate. Time horizon is how long you expect to hold or evaluate. Risk tolerance controls concentration and volatility tolerance. Max position size limits single-name exposure. Cash reserve keeps dry powder. Current holdings helps identify concentration and overlap.
